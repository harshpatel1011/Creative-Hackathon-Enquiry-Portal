Separate Admin and Counselor dashboards with distinct permissions. Admins manage everything; counselors manage their own enquiries.

Add, edit, view and delete student enquiries. Track source, course interest, education qualification, and all contact details.

Add timestamped follow-up notes to every enquiry. Track student status from New → Contacted → Interested → Converted.
